      Setup guide

      ALbarajControl F450 simulator

      You can fly right away in simulation mode — no controller needed. Connect a Pixhawk (with its RC receiver) to fly with your real remote controller or run PX4 hardware-in-the-loop.

      ADYaw left / right
      ↑↓Pitch forward / back
      ←→Roll left / right
      ShiftFull stick deflection
      SpaceArm / disarm (double-press in flight)
